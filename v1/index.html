    Executive overview

    Announcements at the event center on one idea: AI experiences are only as useful as the governed data, shared business definitions and operational controls underneath them. Six themes recur across the sources.

    1. Trusted context for Copilot and agents

      Fabric IQ brings governed Power BI semantic models and business definitions into Microsoft Copilot, data agents and ontologies, so answers reuse measures the organization already certifies.

    2. From reports to applications

      Agentic app creation in Power BI Desktop and Fabric Apps aim to turn insight into workflows with write-back on governed data. Several parts are still preview or coming soon.

    3. One copy, many engines

      OneLake shortcuts, mirroring, table APIs and Iceberg-based federation target cross-platform access with less duplication, while OneLake security continues to enforce access.

    4. Agents for builders and operators

      A data engineering agent, Copilot in Real-Time Intelligence, operations agents and database agents extend AI to people who build and run the platform, with human oversight stated by Microsoft.

    5. Governance that travels with the data

      Policies, DLP restrict access, outbound access protection and Copilot controls extend administrative guardrails to new AI experiences.

    6. Visibility and cost controls

      Observability (workspace monitoring, refreshed Monitor hub), capacity overage, surge protection and upcoming on-demand billing address reliability and spend. Pricing is not stated in the sources.
